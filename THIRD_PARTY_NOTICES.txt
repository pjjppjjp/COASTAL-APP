## External map and search services

These services are requested at runtime and are not included as datasets in the source distribution:

- **Esri World Imagery:** imagery supplied by Esri and its contributors. Retain the provider credits displayed on the map. See the [World Imagery service](https://services.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer) for service metadata and attribution.
- **OpenStreetMap:** map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. See [OpenStreetMap copyright and licensing](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) and the [tile usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/). Retain visible attribution.
- **Photon:** location search from [Photon](https://github.com/komoot/photon), based on OpenStreetMap data. The public service is subject to its published usage conditions and has no uptime guarantee. Photon server software is not bundled with this app.
- **Google Fonts:** the interface requests DM Sans and Manrope through Google Fonts. These font files are served externally rather than included in the source distribution. Font licensing information is available through [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/knowledge/using_type/licensing).
